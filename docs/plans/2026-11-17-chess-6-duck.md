# Chess Plan 6: Duck Chess

**Goal:** Duck Chess as a curated variant, played as a piece move then a duck move, against every AI level and hot-seat.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/chess/chess.md` (Variants, Piece art: Duck Chess, the duck), `editor.md` (Duck Chess bases), `tutorial.md` (Duck Chess).

**Facts (probed 2026-11-17, ffish 0.7.10 and Fairy-Stockfish 1.1.12):** ffish's `duck` variant starts with no duck; a move is `<move>,<piece-to><duck-square>` (`e7e5,e5a3`: pawn to e5, duck to a3); the duck is `*` in the FEN; SAN is `e5,a3`; the engine plays it legally. chessgroundx reads `*` as role `_-piece` (its drop origin is `*@`), colour black.

**Global constraints:** as in Plans 1–5 (yarn, TDD, layer rule, no mocks of first-party code, the UI never decides legality, no Claude signature lines). The duck squares offered come only from the rules' legal moves. Nothing assumes a single duck.

## Tasks

1. **Variant and moves.** Catalog entry `duck` ("Duck Chess", 8×8). `parseUci` reads the duck part (`duck: string | null`, the duck's square). `parsePieceClass` and the art read the duck role (`_-piece`); duck art in `public/pieces/duck/duck.svg`, the same in every set. Coverage and variant-page tests accept the variant. Tests: parsing, art, and real-rules integration (no duck at the start, a duck move, a king capture ends the game).
2. **The two-step move.** After a piece move whose candidates carry a duck part, the board shows the position with the piece moved and the duck where it was (or waiting beside the board on the first turn), lights up every duck that may move, and offers only the duck squares of those candidates; moving a duck plays the paired move. Escape cancels; a promotion asks before the duck moves; the AI's moves play directly. Tests: e2e for the first duck placement, a later duck move, cancelling, undo, the AI's reply, and a king capture winning.
3. **Editor and tutorial.** Position-only Duck Chess base (the chess pieces and the duck in the palette, played as curated `duck`). A Duck Chess variant page. Tests: editor e2e (place the duck, Play) and the content checks.
